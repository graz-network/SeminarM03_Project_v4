# Grading checklist translated into workflow rules

## 1. Project setup and research design (20%)
The report should make it obvious that:
- the research questions are explicit and coherent;
- the replication goal is clearly stated;
- the extension follows logically from the original paper;
- dataset choice and variable construction are justified;
- preprocessing decisions are documented.

## 2. Code quality and reproducibility (20%)
The project should leave behind:
- runnable scripts rather than opaque manual steps;
- a stable folder structure;
- clear filenames and outputs;
- minimal but sufficient documentation;
- reproducible replication and extension modules that connect cleanly.

## 3. Econometric methods and implementation (25%)
The analysis must show:
- why the RD design is appropriate;
- how assumptions and limitations are handled;
- how bandwidth, polynomial order, and clustering choices affect interpretation;
- robustness checks and sensitivity analyses;
- disciplined language when identification weakens.

## 4. Interpretation and critical discussion (20%)
The report should do more than restate coefficients. It should:
- interpret magnitudes economically;
- compare replication and extension meaningfully;
- discuss uncertainty, sample loss, and measurement issues;
- identify what is supported strongly, weakly, or not at all.

## 5. Report quality (15%)
The report should be:
- well structured and concise;
- visually clear;
- academically written;
- consistent in references, tables, and terminology;
- evidently grounded in the original paper rather than generic commentary.

## Practical check before submission
Use this quick audit before finalizing:
1. Can another person run the project from the workspace without guessing paths?
2. Are the main replication tables matched to the original paper?
3. Is the extension question explicit and econometrically operationalized?
4. Are tables and figures cited in the text and actually used in the argument?
5. Is every strong claim tied to a specific specification?
6. Is the 15-page limit respected?
